Dropbox

Not connected. One-time setup, about 5 minutes:
1) Go to dropbox.com/developers/apps → Create app → Scoped access → Full Dropbox → name it (e.g. "Clore DayLog").
2) On the app's Permissions tab, check files.content.read and files.content.write, then Submit.
3) On the Settings tab, under Development users, click Enable additional users.
4) Copy the App key (Settings tab) and paste it below.
Your data saves to Dropbox / Clore DayLog — entries in App Data, receipt photos in Receipts. Nothing goes anywhere else.
